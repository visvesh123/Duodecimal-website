# Privacy Policy

Duodecimal Tech LLP

Last Updated: October, 2026

## Introduction

This Privacy Policy (the "Policy") sets out the manner in which Duodecimal Tech LLP, a limited liability partnership constituted under the laws of India and having its office at Hyderabad, Telangana, India ("Duodecimal", the "Company", "we", "us" or "our"), collects, uses, discloses, stores, transfers and otherwise processes Personal Data in connection with its website, applications, products, services, application programming interfaces and other offerings (collectively, the "Services").

The Company is committed to processing Personal Data lawfully, fairly and transparently, and in accordance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the rules made thereunder (together, the "DPDP Act"), the Information Technology Act, 2000, and all other laws, rules and regulations for the time being in force in India that apply to the Company (collectively, "Applicable Law").

In this Policy, "Personal Data" means any data about an individual who is identifiable by or in relation to such data, and "Data Principal" means the individual to whom the Personal Data relates. Capitalised terms not defined in this Policy have the meanings given to them under Applicable Law.

You are requested to read this Policy carefully before accessing or using the Services. If you do not agree with any part of this Policy, you must discontinue use of the Services.

## 1. Scope and Roles of the Parties

**1.1 Applicability.** This Policy applies to all visitors, users, customers and other individuals who access or use the Services.

**1.2 Exclusions.** This Policy does not apply to any third-party website, product or service that is not owned or controlled by the Company, whether or not it is linked to, or accessible through, the Services.

**1.3 Processing on behalf of an organisation.** Where an organisation makes the Services available to its users, that organisation determines the purpose and means of processing the Personal Data of such users and acts as the Data Fiduciary. In such cases, the Company acts as a Data Processor and processes Personal Data solely on behalf of, and in accordance with the lawful instructions of, that organisation and the agreement between the Company and that organisation. The organisation is responsible for providing notice to, and obtaining any consent required from, the Data Principals concerned.

**1.4 Direct use of the Services.** Where an individual uses the Company's own products or Services directly, the Company acts as the Data Fiduciary in respect of the Personal Data processed for that use.

**1.5 Clarification of roles.** If you are uncertain which of the above applies to you, you may contact the Company using the details in Section 15.

## 2. Personal Data Processed

Depending on the manner in which you interact with the Services, the Company may process the following categories of Personal Data.

### 2.1 Data Provided by You

- Name, email address and telephone number
- Organisation or company details and designation
- Account and authentication information, including one-time passcodes (OTP) used for sign-in
- Communications with the Company, enquiries and feedback
- Files, documents or other content that you elect to submit

### 2.2 Data Collected Automatically

- Internet Protocol (IP) address, device information, browser type, operating system and language preferences
- Date and time of access, pages viewed and usage statistics
- Log files and diagnostic information
- Information collected through cookies and similar technologies (see Section 4)

### 2.3 Data Generated Through Use of the Services

- Inputs, requests, uploaded content and generated outputs
- Interaction history, service preferences and usage metrics
- Technical metadata

### 2.4 Data Received from an Organisation

Where an organisation makes the Services available to you, it may furnish to the Company information relating to you, such as your name, contact details, role and any identifiers assigned to you by that organisation. The Company may assign a unique identifier to you so that your records remain correctly associated.

### 2.5 Data Accessed Through Device Permissions

Where an application or Service requests access to certain device features, such access is requested only when a feature requires it and is used only for the purpose stated at the time of the request:

- **Camera:** used solely to capture images or video that you elect to provide for the feature in use.
- **Location:** used solely to provide location-related functionality that you request, and only while that feature is in use.
- **Notifications:** where you permit notifications, a notification token is stored to enable the Company to send you announcements, reminders and service messages.
- **Files and storage:** used solely when you elect to upload, save or share content.

You may withdraw any such permission at any time through your device settings. Features that depend on a permission may not function without it.

The Company processes Personal Data only for lawful purposes, and only on the basis of your consent or another ground recognised as a lawful use under Applicable Law, including compliance with legal obligations. Where an organisation is the Data Fiduciary, that organisation is responsible for establishing the lawful basis for its own purposes.

The Company may process Personal Data for the following purposes:

- Providing, operating and maintaining the Services
- Creating and authenticating accounts and users
- Sending notifications that you have permitted, and communicating service updates and important notices

- Processing transactions, where applicable
- Monitoring, safeguarding and improving the security, functionality and performance of the Services

- Establishing, exercising or defending legal claims and protecting the rights, property and safety of the Company, its users and others

The Company shall not process Personal Data for any purpose that is incompatible with the foregoing without informing you and, where required by Applicable Law, obtaining your consent.

## 5. Disclosure of Personal Data

The Company may disclose Personal Data only to the extent reasonably necessary, and only to the following categories of recipients:

- The organisation that makes the Services available to you, in accordance with the roles it assigns
- Service providers and Data Processors engaged by the Company, who are bound by written obligations to safeguard Personal Data and to process it only for the services rendered to the Company
- Business partners that support the delivery of the Services

- Affiliates of the Company

## 6. Security Safeguards

The Company implements reasonable security safeguards, including appropriate administrative, technical and organisational measures, to protect Personal Data in its possession or under its control against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure, loss or destruction.

Notwithstanding the foregoing, no method of electronic transmission or storage is entirely secure, and the Company cannot guarantee absolute security. You are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of your credentials and passcodes.

**Personal Data Breach.** In the event of a Personal Data breach affecting you, the Company shall give intimation to the Data Protection Board of India and to affected Data Principals in the manner and within the time prescribed by Applicable Law. Where the Company acts as a Data Processor, it shall inform the relevant Data Fiduciary without undue delay to enable it to discharge its obligations.

## 7. Retention and Erasure

The Company retains Personal Data only for as long as is necessary to fulfil the purposes set out in this Policy, to perform its contractual obligations, to resolve disputes, to enforce its rights, and to comply with Applicable Law. Thereafter, Personal Data is erased or anonymised. Certain information, including certain logs, may be retained for such minimum period as Applicable Law requires.

- Data processed on behalf of an organisation is retained for the period instructed by that organisation and as provided in the agreement between the Company and that organisation. Such data is erased only upon the organisation's instruction, unless Applicable Law requires otherwise. On cessation of use of the Services by an organisation, the Company shall return such data in a usable format or continue to hold it, as the applicable agreement provides.
- Account information in the Company's own products is retained while the account remains active and is erased or anonymised within a reasonable period after closure of the account, unless retention is required by Applicable Law.

## 8. Cross-Border Transfers

Personal Data may be processed or stored in jurisdictions outside India. Any such transfer shall be made in accordance with the DPDP Act and subject to any restriction notified by the Central Government, and the Company shall implement appropriate contractual and security safeguards in respect of such transfer.

## 9. Rights of Data Principals

Subject to Applicable Law, a Data Principal has the right to:

- Access a summary of the Personal Data being processed and the processing activities undertaken, together with the identities of the Data Fiduciaries and Data Processors with whom the Personal Data has been shared
- Correction, completion and updating of Personal Data
- Erasure of Personal Data, where it is no longer necessary for the purpose for which it was processed or consent has been withdrawn, unless retention is required by Applicable Law
- Withdrawal of consent at any time. Withdrawal shall be as easy as the giving of consent, shall not affect the lawfulness of processing undertaken before withdrawal, and may result in the Company being unable to provide features that depend on such consent
- Grievance redressal in respect of any act or omission of the Company relating to the Data Principal's Personal Data or rights (see Section 15)
- Nomination of another individual to exercise these rights in the event of death or incapacity

Depending on your place of residence, additional rights may be available to you under local law.

**Exercise of rights.** Where you access the Services through an organisation, you are requested to address your request to that organisation in the first instance, as it controls your records. The Company shall forward any such request it receives to the relevant organisation and shall reasonably support its response. In respect of the Company's own products, you may delete your account within the application or by writing to the Company at the address specified in Section 15. The Company may require reasonable verification of identity before acting on any request.

If you are dissatisfied with the Company's response, you may approach the Data Protection Board of India, after having exhausted the grievance redressal process described in Section 15.
